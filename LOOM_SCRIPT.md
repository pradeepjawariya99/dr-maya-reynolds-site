# Loom demo script (about 5 minutes, ~650 words)

Speak to Dr. Maya as the client. Plain language, no tech terms. Make it yours; don't read it word for word.

**0:00 Intro (desktop, top of page)**
"Hi Dr. Reynolds, this is the first draft of your new website. I'll show you the desktop and mobile versions, why I made the choices I did, and how I used your profile."

**0:30 Hero**
"The very first thing a visitor reads is: Anxiety & trauma therapy in Santa Monica. That's what you do and where you are, so people searching for a therapist near them can find you, and they know within seconds they're in the right place. The line under it, 'for when you're tired of bracing for the worst,' comes from how you describe your clients. The two arched images feel like windows, which keeps it calm and welcoming."

**1:15 Colours and feel**
"I chose deep blue-teal, sea-glass green, and a touch of sunset amber, inspired by Santa Monica at dusk. Blue-teal feels steady and trustworthy, and amber is used only for the button so your call to action is always easy to spot. Text is high contrast so it's comfortable to read."

**2:00 Scroll: 'Who I help' and focus areas**
"These three cards speak directly to the people you described: adults with anxiety and panic, people healing from trauma, and professionals facing burnout. Every word here is taken from your profile. The list of focus areas helps visitors quickly see, 'she understands what I'm going through.'"

**2:50 About you**
"Here is your photo with a short bio. I highlighted the methods you use, CBT, EMDR, mindfulness, and body-oriented work, so people see your approach at a glance."

**3:20 Services**
"Three services: anxiety and panic, trauma therapy with EMDR, and burnout and perfectionism. Each has a clear description, and the location is included naturally, which helps you show up in local searches."

**3:50 Our Office (new section)**
"I added a section showing your Santa Monica office. Seeing the space in advance helps nervous first-time clients feel safe. It also states that you offer in-person sessions and secure online sessions for clients in California."

**4:20 FAQs and booking**
"The FAQs answer common questions before someone has to ask. And the final section makes booking simple: one clear button."

**4:40 Mobile**
[Switch to phone view] "Most people will find you on their phone, so everything stacks neatly, text stays easy to read, and the menu opens with one tap."

**4:55 Close**
"That's the first draft. Tell me what you'd like to adjust: colours, photos, or wording. Thank you!"
